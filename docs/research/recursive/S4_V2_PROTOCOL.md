# S4-v2 precommitted protocol — transition-informed successor (public synthetic development only)

Status: PRE-SCORE protocol. Previous S4-v1 negative result remains unchanged. This is a new hypothesis, not an independent confirmatory trial.

Hypothesis: A bounded successor that learns the sign of temporal autocorrelation of task families from earlier development tasks improves *future unseen-seed* cumulative reward by >= 5 percentage points relative to the strongest matched online adaptive, static-repeat, static-flip, and alternating controls. Negative-control reversal must lose no more than 5 percentage points relative to the strongest matched control. Failure of either criterion is REJECT. No retuning or seed selection after results.

Four generations; 32 tasks per generation; six labels per task; five policies; public seeds: development 20261106+g, future 20262106+g, adversarial 20263106+g. Hidden binary task type follows Markov transition stay probability 0.85 on development and future; adversarial stay probability 0.15. Arm rewards are Bernoulli 0.8 for matching arm and 0.2 otherwise. Each policy has six evaluator labels per task. Every policy receives the same task worlds and pre-generated potential outcomes (common random numbers). Do not reveal latent type to learner.

All policies use the same within-task two-arm Beta(1,1) greedy update after two forced exploration pulls. Their only difference is which arm is selected for the first pull and tie-breaking: (1) successor learned transition mode from inferred previous-task types on development only; (2) online-last: predict previous task inferred type; (3) online-flip: predict opposite; (4) fixed0; (5) fixed1. Each policy infers previous task type solely from its own within-task receipts, selecting the arm with higher empirical success fraction; ties retain its first-pull arm. The successor estimates transition persistence from inferred consecutive types on development tasks under its own online-last probe policy, with a Laplace prior; if estimated stay probability >= 0.5, predict same, else flip. This learned bit is frozen for future and negative evaluation. No access to future latent types, negative outcomes, or future rewards for learning the transition bit. Online controls use same last-task feedback and budgets.

All policies evaluated on every generation/split with no early exclusion. Development probe labels count toward total cost. Total planned labels: 4*32*6*5*3=11520, with successor development probe reused for successor policy (no extra paid labels). The candidate changes a policy bit, not self-authored code or architecture.

Report full pooled success counts, generation-by-generation counts, inferred learned bits, total simulated labels, wall/CPU timing, and adversarial inversion. Acceptance requires >= 0.05*(4*32*6)=38.4 successes above the best future control and >= -38.4 successes versus best negative control. No statistical significance claim from a single seed; regardless of outcome, scientific status remains NOT_ASSESSED beyond public synthetic development.
