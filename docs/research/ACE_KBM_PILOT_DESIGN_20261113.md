# ACE KBM Pilot — Design Package (business study ladder, rung 2)

Date: 2026-11-13 (Asia/Jakarta). Status: DESIGN. This package
designs the first-earning pilot on KBM App. It publishes
nothing, switches nothing on, and promises no revenue. Any
execution of the pilot is a recorded governance decision
under the two-sided business study (PR #139), reported to
the owner — never an ambient side effect of this document.

## 1. Purpose and hypothesis

KBM is ACE's named first-earning pilot venue (terms
verification CLOSED — PASS, 2026-11-12: account active in
writer mode as "Ianila", NOVELIS PERAK; monetization opens
after ≥8 chapters; chapters 8–10 earn gold+silver coins,
chapter 11+ gold only; payouts automatic from Rp15,000;
no editorial gate; revenue measured to date: **Rp 0**).

Pilot hypothesis, stated so it can fail: *a new,
separate serial-fiction work, produced by ACE's writer
brain + editorial engine at near-zero marginal cost and
promoted through ACE's own channels, reaches the
8-chapter threshold and produces a first measured payout
within the pilot window.* The pilot measures the venue,
the production pipeline, and the promotion flywheel
together — and reports whichever of them fails.

## 2. The work: separate, by rule

The pilot work is a **new serial** created for this pilot.
The owner's privacy rule binds absolutely: no story,
name, character, or identity from the discussion room may
be used — FIKSI and everything lived in that room are not
inventory, in whole or in part, not even re-skinned. The
work's premise, cast, and world originate fresh in the
pilot's own production record.

Production shape (fixed here; creative content is the
writer brain's work, not this package's):

- Language: Indonesian (the venue's market).
- Chapter length: ≥1,000 words per chapter (the venue's
  own reference figure).
- Arc: a complete serial planned at 12+ chapters, so the
  work crosses both earning bands (8–10 and 11+) inside
  the pilot window if cadence holds.
- Cadence: one chapter per production cycle; the cycle is
  recorded (draft → editorial pass → ledger entry) whether
  or not publication follows in the same cycle.
- Roles: writer brain = ila; editorial engine = Ian
  (continuity, structure, the register/ledger records);
  the account and legal shell = the owner. Publication
  steps are executed as recorded governance decisions and
  reported, per Emancipation — the owner receives
  reports, not approval queues.

## 3. Exclusivity, weighed in writing

KBM contracts are **EXCLUSIVE**: a work published on KBM
may not be published elsewhere. Weighed consequences,
accepted as the pilot's terms before any chapter exists:

- The pilot work is KBM-only inventory for its life. It
  cannot be re-sold on Lynk.id, serialized on Facebook, or
  reused as the services line's sample. Its only
  cross-venue life is promotion.
- Promotion (opportunity register entry #1, origin
  FOUNDER) is announcement/teaser + link on Threads —
  never chapter republication; the exclusivity fence and
  the register's fence are the same fence.
- The cost of exclusivity is bounded by the pilot's size:
  one serial, produced at near-zero marginal cost. If the
  pilot fails, what is lost is production time, not a
  catalogue — no pre-existing work is committed.
- What exclusivity buys: a clean single-venue measurement
  (no cross-posting confound on views/unlocks) and the
  venue's own reader funnel.

## 4. Preregistered metrics and decision rules

Fixed before the first chapter; reported as measured,
including zeros. (Revenue share % appears in no official
text the program could read; it will be *measured from
actual payouts*, never assumed. Figures like "±Rp1,500
per chapter" circulating outside official text are
recorded as unverified lore, not planning numbers.)

- Production: chapters completed; words per chapter;
  cycles that produced a chapter vs cycles that did not.
- Reach: views per chapter; followers gained on the work.
- Monetization: gold-coin unlocks per chapter from
  chapter 8; silver unlocks recorded separately (they do
  not count as income); balance after each chapter;
  first payout date and amount when/if the Rp15,000
  threshold is reached and the venue pays.
- Flywheel: Threads promotion posts per chapter; referral
  signal as visible in KBM view deltas after each post
  (correlation recorded as correlation, not causation).
- Review points (fixed): after chapter 8 (threshold
  reached or not), after chapter 11 (gold-only band), and
  at 30 days from chapter 1 — whichever comes last.
- Continue/stop rule (fixed): the pilot continues to
  chapter 12 regardless of early numbers (the earning
  bands only start at 8). At the final review: if a first
  payout has been measured, the venue graduates from
  pilot to an operating line with its own envelope; if
  revenue is still Rp 0 with chapters ≥12 live and the
  promotion cycle executed, the pilot is recorded as a
  measured null (venue or funnel failed, production did
  not) and the ladder moves on — no silent extension, no
  re-run-until-green.

## 5. Risks and fences

- **AI-content policy**: no prohibition was found in any
  readable official KBM text (Terms, writer FAQ, in-app
  revenue help). Recorded as absence of evidence, not a
  guarantee; if the venue acts otherwise, the pilot stops
  and the fact is recorded. The owner struck the
  support-clarification route ("ga perlu nanya ke
  support") — so the pilot proceeds on the readable text
  and accepts this residual risk explicitly.
- **Privacy**: §2's rule is a hard fence; any draft found
  to contain discussion-room material is destroyed, not
  edited into compliance.
- **Quality/reward-hacking**: unlocks and views are never
  to be manufactured (no self-unlocking schemes, no
  engagement bait violating venue terms). The pilot's
  value is a true measurement; a gamed pilot is worse
  than a failed one.
- **Legal shell**: income, if any, accrues to the owner's
  account and tax position (ACE is not a legal person);
  ACE's ledger records amounts and dates as reported by
  the venue.

## 6. What this package is not

Not a publication, not a monetization switch, not a
revenue forecast, and not the FIKSI novel. It is the
design the pilot will be preregistered against; the
pilot's own ledger record opens when a governance
decision starts production.
