# E1.4p — pre-score typed inheritance into a branching weighted graph

Use 12 fresh symbolic source blocks, four per motif length seven, eight and nine. Each source receives scripted negative and positive evidence as in E1.4n. The isolated predecessor must actually fit a unique binary sign motif after the old five/six grammar is empty. A typed artifact contains the selected word and a digest of its source evidence and learned word. A fixed human-authored mapping converts increasing/decreasing to same/different adjacent edge labels. The successor is a fresh isolated process and receives neither source inputs nor held-out probes.

The new task is minimum path cost on a branching weighted directed acyclic graph. Inputs have four routes with shared vertices and may have multiple matching paths of different costs. For each source lineage freeze a paired target: aligned edge motif or its binary complement. Both share four negative graph demos and eight held-out graph probes. The same optional one-label calibration graph activates the aligned motif and excludes its complement. Six additional shifted worlds receive a neutral calibration graph which activates neither. All raw fingerprints are fresh. No target labels enter the source fit. This is a different task and interpreter with a known shared motif, and the mapping is supplied.

At zero and one target label, compare a cold exhaustive exact fit, the learned artifact, and a sham artifact with its first event flipped. Each candidate receives identical target evidence. The successor uses an artifact only while it agrees with every observed target label, otherwise reverts to the exact cold set. Score only unanimous nonempty predictions on eight held-out graphs. Retain false positives, incorrect accepted predictions, abstentions, label counts, and candidate fits separately. The source fitting budget and human-authored mapping are part of the experiment. Match target label budgets, while reporting compute differences and source observation cost.

**Aligned transfer GO** requires all 12 predecessor artifacts, at least 10/12 aligned complete correct worlds with one target label, at least six more than cold and strictly more than sham, plus complete rejection of incompatible inheritance on shifted-visible worlds with exact fallback to the cold set. Shift-hidden is a stress control for insufficient evidence and must be reported separately. Keep NATURAL_HOLD for unconstrained transfer. Freeze all sources, raw data and thresholds before scoring. Run bounded isolated score and independent replay, audit lineage and graph outputs, and checkpoint each lineage.
