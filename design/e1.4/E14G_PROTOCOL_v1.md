# E1.4g public development protocol v1

**Parent** [E1.4f checkpoint](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/b34b316924034b73f9b7ff1c09c1c987e9b5a688). **Question** Can a benchmark detect the value of acquiring one label and expose a fixed representational limit under separate input distributions?

- The **natural synthetic** distribution has 24 blocks. Independently of the hidden target word, preallocate 32 demonstrations, 8 pool items and 8 probes from random 9–14-event traces. Draw the length-5 or length-6 motif uniformly without replacement within each family/length. Enforce exact raw-input deduplication against the frozen prior public inventory and within this bank. No requirement for a positive, disagreement or solvable case. This distribution describes the generator alone.
- The **opportunity-enriched** distribution has 24 blocks. Choose two motifs per block. Render a negative cover for all other same-length motifs, plus padding; two pool traces distinguish the motifs and six agree. All pool traces within a block have equal event count. Shuffle pool order before scoring. The eight probes are four renderings of each of two witness traces. Each block has two mirrored worlds with identical raw inputs and different target motifs. Aggregate these worlds as a single block for gates. The separate generator changes all three input roles, so its difference from the natural distribution cannot identify the causal effect of enriching the query pool alone.
- Two families, six blocks per distribution × family × length cell. **S** predicts a bit at each event boundary for an exact contiguous sign/equality word. **G** predicts total cost for an ordered single forward chain containing an exact word of equal/changed adjacent edge labels, otherwise null. G excludes the first-edge sentinel. These are new public motifs and renderings under a restricted grammar, with distinct motifs within each family/length across the two distributions. Exact deduplication covers the [recorded E1.4 public inventory](../../evidence/e14/e14g_prior_inputs_v1.json.gz). The prior frozen E1.4f generated bank SHA was `d79acfec7a9778bae4f2e6599a00cdbe5b3e22b4a13d269fa74bf7d54bdfbf2b`. Related/unrelated in the execution ledger denote represented length 5 and out-of-basis length 6 here.
- Fix all six slots per cell. No outcome filtering, replacement, seed tuning, cross-world learning, or cross-episode transfer. Freeze bank, prior input digest index, protocol, source and tests in Git before scoring. Scoring and replay use public data only. A worker sees labeled demonstrations and either unlabeled pool or unlabeled probes. A one-label fit receives just the selected additional labeled example. The evaluator privately forms labels and scores exact whole-probe outputs.
- Five-event learner enumerates every word, maximizes demo matches, breaks fit ties lexicographically, and forms a committee from all best fits. Comparators use **no label**, **one fixed label** from the longest trace with index tie break, **exact random expectation** over all eight choices, **Max-Gini** (minimize sum of squared partition sizes), **minimax** (minimize largest partition, favor more partitions on ties), and **hindsight** (best of all eight, retrospective only). Acquisition ties prefer lowest frozen pool index. The **informed reference** gets the true grammar length, never the true word, selects by Max-Gini and receives one extra label. Different class sizes mean different enumeration work. No training amortization or compute-parity claim.
- Out-of-basis witnesses are designed for at most 4/8 exact probes for *every* five-event word in each mirrored world. S twins share a five-event suffix. G twins have identical sets of length-five windows and distinct relevant six-event windows. Verify the ceiling exhaustively before scoring, including the case where competing length-five hypotheses both fail. Retain harm and unanimous committees in raw evidence, including exact negative results.
- Count labels separately from worker budgets. Each world uses 32 demonstration, 8 query, and 8 probe oracle evaluations. Each deployed one-label comparator gets 32 demo labels and 1 query label; the exhaustive oracle measurement pays for all eight. Mirrored worlds share inputs but have distinct labels. The worker runs under native Bubblewrap, per-call 1 CPU second, 3 wall seconds and 128 MiB address space, minimal read-only code mount and a fresh network namespace. Record binary SHA, canary results and per-call costs. Stop if isolation or freeze integrity fails. Use transactionally resumable world rows with explicit run timeout and terminal logs. Score twice from separate ledgers; replay compares deterministic evidence, excluding per-call resource timing/RSS.
- **Engineered development GO** requires, for each in-basis opportunity family, paired block improvement on at least 5/6 blocks for hindsight over no-label, Max-Gini over exact random and minimax over exact random; both active policies must beat the static one-label and no-label controls on total probes. For each out-of-basis opportunity family, aggregate hindsight at most 4 correct probes/world and informed over hindsight in at least 5/6 paired blocks. A failure yields `BENCHMARK_HOLD_REDESIGN`. The gate permits a broader development benchmark only.
- **Natural development GO** separately requires, in each in-basis family, hindsight over no-label in ≥3/6 blocks, Max-Gini over no-label in ≥2/6, and Max-Gini above exact random in total. Otherwise `NATURAL_HOLD`. The two length-6 natural cells are reported descriptively. Neither distribution rescues the other. No inference about autonomous invention, transfer, or confirmation follows from these gates. Preserve all cells, predictions, comparison counts and checks.
