# E1.4p — motif inheritance into a branching graph

**Aligned transfer GO. NATURAL_HOLD.** The frozen protocol is at [9fe13a1](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/9fe13a1b7e8087c2b0e2f3b1209f4f8d6499605b). This is public development evidence.

Each of 12 isolated symbolic predecessors fit a unique motif of seven to nine events and exported a typed artifact with its source evidence digest. The 30 target worlds used a fresh isolated successor and a distinct interpreter for minimum cost over four graph routes. It received graph demos and, depending on the arm, the artifact. A human-supplied mapping from symbolic signs to relative edge labels made the representations compatible. The source used 384 initial demonstrations plus 40 scheduled observations. Target budgets were matched at zero or one calibration label per arm.

| Target and budget | Inherited | Cold exhaustive | Sham |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Aligned, 0 labels, worlds fully correct | 12/12 | 0/12 | 0/12 |
| Aligned, 1 label, worlds fully correct | 12/12 | 1/12 | 1/12 |
| Visible shift, 1 label, artifacts accepted | 0/12 | — | 10/12 |
| Visible shift, 1 label, wrong accepted probes | 0/96 | 0/96 | 40/96 |
| Hidden shift, 1 label, wrong accepted probes | 48/48 | 0/48 | 26/48 |

On aligned worlds with one calibration label, the artifact covered all 96 probes with zero errors and used 12 candidate fits, versus 49 covered probes and 10,752 fits for cold. In all 12 visible shifts, the calibration contradicted the inherited artifact and restored the exact cold candidate set. Cold and inherited both covered four probes and abstained on 92. With no calibration, the real artifact accepted all 96 visible-shift probes incorrectly. In the six hidden shifts the neutral calibration failed to expose the mismatch. The inherited policy accepted 48 wrong predictions, while cold abstained on all 48. These are designed stress strata, not shift prevalence estimates.

Score and independent replay matched on the audited deterministic projection across 12 lineages and 30 worlds. The audit reconstructed source evidence and artifact identity, enumerated graph paths, and checked cold and artifact predictions. Native Bubblewrap boundary checks and all 108 recovered E1.4 regression tests passed. [Verification](../../evidence/e14/e14p_verification_v1.json).

The GO covers reuse of a learned typed artifact under a known cross-task mapping. The experiment leaves unconstrained transfer and shift detection on HOLD. A successor should buy a diagnostic observation whose competing outcomes disagree before trusting an artifact on a shifted task.
