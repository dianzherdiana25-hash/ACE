# E1.4k — pre-score compound coverage protocol

**Parent** [E1.4j result](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/8c86e029d4483871302484b315808be8137d5a4b) · [Issue #4](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/issues/4).

- Keep the exact length-five and length-six grammars and separate symbolic sequences (S) from graph chains (G). Six natural blocks per true length and family produce 24 worlds. Natural input generation precedes target sampling. Each family also has six packable and six ceiling blocks, each with two mirrored targets and the same raw inputs. There are 48 blocks and 72 worlds. The 32 demos and eight probes per block use fresh raw inputs against the prior public inventory. All held-out probes have 14 events, outside every query arm. Motifs and event shapes may recur.
- In packable cases, the demo cover leaves three compatible short words that never occupy consecutive windows of a six-event query. At least three long words remain. An independently sampled 12-event certificate contains all three short words but excludes the long target. The target long word contains at least one short word to preserve negative demo consistency. A blank answer to the certificate rules out every short word. The worker does not receive the certificate. In ceiling cases nine compatible short words and at least eight compatible long words remain. A 12-event query has only eight short and seven long windows, so a shared blank outcome remains possible regardless of the offered query. A specific positive answer can still resolve an individual world.
- Compare E1.4j short guided proposals of five or six events, uniform random 12-event proposals, and guided 12-event compound proposals. Compounds stitch compatible words using maximal suffix-prefix overlap, skip words that do not fit, and pad to 12 events. Guided compounds consider every ordering of a short class of up to three words first, then sample mixed words. At most 32 compound or 64 random draw attempts feed up to eight unique scored inputs per round. The short arm reuses the E1.4j seeded offer procedure. All arms exclude labeled event strings, receive up to two labels and use the same E1.4j minimax ranking and tie rules. The event cost is measured independently and not matched across short and long arms. The long arms have equal event length per offered or purchased question.
- Only the chosen query label is exposed. The worker receives no target, probe, class identifier beyond family, condition, or certificate. Exact fit over both classes determines diagnosis. If both remain possible, report abstention and score the short fallback separately for false positives. Record labels, purchased and offered events, coverage, candidates eliminated, probe accuracy, wrong diagnosis and abstention for caps zero, one and two.
- **COMPOUND_DEVELOPMENT_GO** requires, in each family, correct diagnosis of at least 4/6 packable long worlds under cap one, strictly more than both controls. The aggregate number of short candidates eliminated in these long worlds must also exceed each control under cap one. No wrong compound diagnosis is allowed. Otherwise return **COMPOUND_HOLD**. Ceiling and natural results are descriptive. **NATURAL_HOLD** remains the transfer decision. No hypothesis, threshold, or bank changes after score.
- Freeze sources and bank in Git before scoring and bind upstream manifests. Use the native worker isolation checks, transactional ledger, bounded score and fresh replay, and an independent arithmetic audit of rankings, coverage, labels and scores.
