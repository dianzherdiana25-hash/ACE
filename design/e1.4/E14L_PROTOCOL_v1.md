# E1.4l — pre-score ranking, query cost and selective risk protocol

**Parent** [E1.4k result](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/c920cf8f06fad14011f7e3a8aa95e03f3a846223) · [Issue #5](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/issues/5).

- Use fresh public inputs, excluding prior fingerprints. The in-model bank has 24 natural worlds and 40 engineered worlds. Each family has five mirrored blocks per packable or ceiling stratum, drawn with the E1.4k construction. Separately construct four out-of-model worlds per family with a seven-event target and 32 demos of at most six events. Four probes activate the seven-event target while every five- and six-event window of those probes has been excluded by the negative demos. This deliberately tests agreement under misspecification and does not estimate real-world frequency. Natural input generation precedes target sampling. All held-out probes have 14 events.
- Every strategy receives the same generator at matched state and seed. Take the first four available E1.4k short guided candidates and first four compound guided candidates after deduplication within each source and removal of labeled event strings. There are at most eight scored inputs per round and at most two purchased labels. Each strategy fits only exact five- and six-event classes from observed demos and query labels. The worker sees no target, probe, condition, or certificate. The candidate emits typed exact words for independent selective evaluation, with the retained evidence binding the lists by digest.
- Compare three rankings. `minimax` uses the E1.4k criterion on this shared mixed pool. `value` ranks by exclusive answer mass divided by cost, then by minimax, shorter length and offer order. Exclusive mass uses equal class prior and uniform weighting within each class and is the estimated probability of eliminating one class. Cost is six event-equivalent units per label plus actual event length. `gate` applies the same value ranking and buys only at value at least 1/36. For a 12-event query this requires exclusive mass at least one half. All policies reject minimax score one, and stop when exactly one class fits, when neither fits, or when no qualifying offer remains. The cost weight and threshold are declared engineering assumptions. Track events and label counts separately.
- Diagnose only a unique remaining class. For forced prediction use the lexicographic best fit in the diagnosed class, otherwise the short class. Separately measure selective prediction on probes by reporting only when the nonempty exact version space agrees on the complete output. Measure coverage, accepted errors, accepted false positives and fallback false positives independently. In-model selective correctness is conditional on the target being one of the exact candidates. Out-of-model evidence is descriptive and excluded from in-model gates.
- **Ranking GO** if on packable long worlds at cap one, value diagnoses at least as many as minimax in each family and more in aggregate. **Budget GO** if at cap two, gate spends at most 80% of minimax purchased events and retains at least 90% of its correct diagnoses in each family across all in-model worlds. Both gates require zero wrong diagnoses across all in-model arms. **Selective PASS** if gate at cap two has zero accepted in-model errors and at least 50% probe coverage per family. Each failing gate receives HOLD. Maintain **NATURAL_HOLD** for transfer.
- Freeze bank, code and thresholds before scoring. Use native Bubblewrap and the upstream manifests, a bounded score and fresh replay, transactional checkpoints, and independent arithmetic audit. Preserve results for all caps, strata and arms, including the out-of-model cases.
