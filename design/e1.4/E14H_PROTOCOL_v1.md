# E1.4h — pre-score routing experiment v1

**Parent** [E1.4g result checkpoint](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/473cda85dd079f7ce951a78e660045603fcb1711). This diagnostic asks whether a fixed learner can identify the need to change representation without being told the target grammar. The two allowed grammars are supplied by the researcher.

- New seed streams, 48 blocks and 72 worlds, same natural and opportunity constructions as [E1.4g](E14G_PROTOCOL_v1.md). Exclude exact prior inputs, including all E1.4g data. Natural motifs exclude E1.4g motifs in the same family and length. Constructed motifs and trace shapes may recur. Do not select or replace blocks after scoring. Keep mirrored worlds within their block.
- Both fixed learners receive the same 32 labeled demos and unlabeled query pool. Retain grammar five unless its best demo fit is imperfect and grammar six fits all 32. Choose the grammar before acquiring a label. If both fit or both fail to fit perfectly, use grammar five and record the condition. Select one question with Max-Gini inside the chosen grammar and fit with that label. Compare fixed five, fixed six, adaptive with no label, and the reference informed about the true length. The oracle does not provide the true length to the adaptive rule.
- A deployed adaptive one-label arm pays for both unlabeled fits and a single additional label. Each fixed one-label control pays for one unlabeled fit and one additional label. No compute-parity claim. The evaluator shares replayable outcomes between controls and charges 32 demo labels, eight probe labels, and one or two distinct pool labels per world. Worker input restrictions, resource limits, and five canaries reuse the frozen E1.4g isolated harness.
- **ROUTING_DEVELOPMENT_GO** if, for both engineered families, at least 5/6 mirrored blocks in each length route both worlds to the true length, length-five adaptive never harms relative to fixed five, length-six adaptive beats fixed five on at least 5/6 blocks and falls at most eight total correct probes below the informed reference. Across the two engineered lengths per family, adaptive must beat both fixed controls in total exact probes. Otherwise `ROUTING_HOLD`. Report all eight cells, wrong routes, neither-perfect counts, and harm. Natural results are descriptive, and the E1.4g natural HOLD remains the decision for transfer.
- Use distinct SQLite ledgers for scoring and replay, with bounded commands and separate arithmetic audit. Reuse fixed trusted workers with no new executable candidate. This public diagnostic can establish routing among two preset grammars under specified conditions. Independent confirmation, learning new primitives, transfer and open-ended evolution are future research.
