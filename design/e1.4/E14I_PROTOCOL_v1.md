# E1.4i — pre-score cross-grammar witness protocol

**Parent** [E1.4h result](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/commit/2004894defcc85489ca7c61a8a901008e16c5e71) · [Issue #2](https://github.com/dianzherdiana25-hash/ACE/issues/2).

- Evaluate two human-supplied grammars for five and six contiguous events in symbolic sequences (S) and graph chains (G). Keep natural synthetic and engineered cases separate. The natural generator preallocates input roles before drawing its word, with six blocks per family and length, 24 total. Use fresh exact inputs against prior public E1.4 fingerprints. Motifs or trace shapes may recur.
- In each of six engineered blocks per family, a six-event word has a five-event suffix. One positive demo fits both words. A negative cover eliminates every other five-event word, and the shared positive eliminates every other six-event word. The two target worlds mirror each other with identical raw inputs and differing labels. Preserve the same 32 demos, 8 probes and 6 of 8 pool inputs between conditions. Replace two mutually negative query items with two short-only witnesses in the witness condition. Keep all pool inputs at six events and share the frozen shuffle. The probes have four short-only examples, two shared positives and two shared negatives. Negative controls keep the same diagnostic probes while withholding the two query witnesses. Treat paired conditions and mirrored worlds as one block for inference about construction.
- The cross-grammar policy buys a label only if both lengths fit all 32 demos and the class predictive distributions disagree on at least one query. Compute total variation distance with equal class weights across the two sets of best-fitting words and choose the highest value with frozen pool order as tie-breaker. When exactly one length fits, diagnose it without buying a label. Diagnose a length after a label only if it is the sole length matching every revealed example. Otherwise report abstention and use the E1.4h demo-selected length as the fallback for probe predictions. Fit ties within each length use lexicographic order.
- Controls are E1.4h (select length from demos, buy one label via within-class Max-Gini), cross-grammar with zero labels, and exact uniform random over all eight one-label branches with the same update rule. Report retrospective maximal exact score, maximal correct diagnosis and minimal false-positive probes across the eight one-label branches as separate bounds. A probe is false positive if any predicted symbolic event contradicts a zero bit, or a graph cost is predicted for a null target. Record exact probe score, correct length, false positive probes, abstentions and labels bought separately. Keep all worlds, both conditions and all branches.
- **WITNESS_DEVELOPMENT_GO** requires, per family, correct diagnosis in both mirrored worlds of at least 5/6 witness blocks and higher aggregate correct diagnosis than E1.4h and exact random. Exact probe score must exceed both controls, and false-positive probes must be fewer than for E1.4h. In the no-witness condition every mirrored world must abstain, buy zero labels and reproduce the zero-label prediction. Otherwise report **WITNESS_HOLD**. Natural cells are descriptive. Count positive distance, retrospectively diagnosable cases, and retrospective gains among ambiguous cases. Carry forward **NATURAL_HOLD** for transfer.
- Freeze all code, bank and rules before scoring. Hash-lock earlier shared sources too. Use the native Bubblewrap boundary, restricted wire with no target word or unqueried labels, run ledger, bounded execution, second ledger replay, and independent arithmetic audit. Worker CPU and memory limits are inherited from E1.4g. Per world the evaluator measures 32 demo labels, 8 query labels and 8 probe labels across branches. Conditions and mirrors share raw inputs, and the execution counts evaluations even for examples repeated across conditions. The selected policy receives at most one query label. Separate worker calls and fit comparisons from oracle and deployed label budgets.
