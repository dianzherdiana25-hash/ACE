# R1 H4 PRE-SCORE protocol: evidence-conditioned counter-register construction

Status: **public synthetic development; pre-score; scientific NOT_ASSESSED**. This protocol is committed before H4 code/scoring. Keep H1–H3, E1.4, main and other teams untouched.

## Research boundary
S2 searches arithmetic programs from provided primitives. H4 instead tests **representation-class expansion**: from finite-state predictions to a learned, potentially unbounded one-integer memory register. The learner derives the register's signed per-symbol increments and acceptance value using equalities between **observed positive examples**, not from a provided target function or a finite list of specific counters. Only the *register construction template* and possible refinement guards are authored in advance. H4 is therefore **not** invention of a new computational primitive class, not novel algorithm discovery, and not OAIE.

## H4 hypothesis and gate
H4a: On eight researcher-generated counter-structured languages, the *train-only evidence-constructed counter with optional residual guard* achieves >=0.85 mean accuracy on balanced, length-disjoint 48-string holdouts **and** improves by >=0.10 absolute over the per-task hindsight-best of original R1 baselines (RPNI, suffix-3 majority, fixed single predicate, and prefix memorizer). Additionally >=6/8 must reach >=0.90 accuracy. All three conditions are required.

H4b (specificity): On three prespecified out-of-class languages (parity of a, avoids aba, palindrome), the counter constructor should abstain/reject a counter hypothesis on >=2/3 while preserving the RPNI fallback as baseline. This is a diagnostic distinct gate; an incorrect or misleading counter proposal is negative evidence.

**Adversarial nonidentifiability control:** a target behaving like #a==#b on training lengths 0..5 but flipping the rule for long lengths. Counter predictions should *fail* under this deliberate unidentifiability test. No numerical H4 acceptance credit is given for success on this control.

## Fixed task families
Training: all 63 binary words of length 0..5 (same immutable original R1 bank), validation: all 192 words of length 6..7 (diagnostic only). Holdout: 24 positives + 24 negatives, *each length >=8*, generated with seed `20261105 + 14000 + task index`, bounded rejection sampling of lengths 8..20, except ordered block tasks (`a^n b^n` and `b^n a^n`) where 24 positive words use n=4..27 and a single length-matched hard negative is generated by shuffling the exact same symbol counts while violating target order.

The eight counter targets:
1. `count(a)==count(b)`.
2. `count(a)==2*count(b)`.
3. `count(b)==2*count(a)`.
4. `count(a)==3*count(b)`.
5. Dyck-1: a=open, b=close, total depth 0 and no negative prefix.
6. Reverse Dyck-1: b=open, a=close, total depth 0 and no negative prefix in reversed register.
7. `a^n b^n` for n>=0.
8. `b^n a^n` for n>=0.

Three out-of-class targets: count(a) odd; avoids substring `aba`; palindrome. A fourth adversarial task equals counts for short strings and negates equality for lengths >=8.

For all non-block target families, holdout positives are sampled with rejection from uniform binary words of random length 8..20; negative examples are sampled at **matched length** and must be false by the target. For Dyck/reverse Dyck, demand *hard negatives with exactly the same a/b counts as matched positives*, to prevent count-only shortcuts. On the equality and ratio families, negatives must have target-count residual of the smallest available nonzero magnitude (1 for equal count; 1 for 2:1 and 3:1) where feasible. Never use validation or holdout observations to construct features or select guards. Generation cap 250,000 attempts per target class: fail visibly if cannot meet all constraints. Every task has 48 holdout evaluations.

## Precommitted representation construction
1. Gather positive training examples and convert **only** each string's observable Parikh vector (#a,#b).
2. For every *pair* of distinct positive Parikh vectors, infer an integer normal to their vector difference using gcd reduction; canonical sign with w_a>0. Keep normals with both coordinates nonzero and opposite signs (to target a balancing relation).
3. Accept a learned register proposal `q(s)=w_a*#a(s)+w_b*#b(s)`, with acceptance `q(s)=t` where t inferred from a positive example, **only if it gives zero false negatives across ALL positive training examples**. If multiple surviving normals, rank by number of false positives on training, then by |w_a|+|w_b| and canonical order; all selection train-only. If none survives, explicitly abstain and use original RPNI.
4. To address counterexamples where q(s)=t predicts false positives, attempt at most one residual guard from researcher-authored *classes*, each instantiated from observed training only: (i) `prefix_min(q)>=threshold` and (ii) `prefix_max(q)<=threshold` with thresholds anchored to extremes of positive-prefix values, and (iii) exclusion of one observed two-character substring absent in every positive training string. Guard must **not reject any training positive**; pick maximum false-positive removal, then simpler deterministic tie order. Guard is chosen only if it removes >=1 training false positive.
5. If errors remain on training, the learned counter is **rejected**; use original fixed-class DFA/RPNI as fallback rather than invent exception tables. Rank/selection from training only; no lookups of target names or holdout labels. Budget at most 1000 register proposals and 1000 guard tests/task.

Constructed register is an **unbounded integer state** during runtime; a fixed 63-node DFA cannot express all its equivalence classes. However its *type*, feature statistic and refinement operators were authored by the researcher. This is only evidence-conditioned synthesis within that template.

## Baselines and accounting
- Existing original R1 RPNI with 63 states / <=10k attempted merges, memorizer, suffix3, best frozen single predicate by training error. Same 63 train labels for all.
- Also show **unrefined inferred counter** as ablation; and a *guarded counter with no abstention* only for diagnostic, to expose false-accept paths.
- Primary oracle baseline is best **holdout score per task** among four baselines, explicitly favoring the controls.
- Validation and holdout are terminal scoring only; calculate accuracy and confusion by task, counts, register coefficients, selected guard, rejection reason and counterexamples.
- No code candidate execution, no paid evaluators, no network requests, no merges.

## Limits and integrity
Counter-structured targets and the candidate register algebra are researcher-defined; positive-only structural inference may be underidentified, and a length-switch target is mathematically indistinguishable from equal-count on train. Tests of **independent alphabets**, arithmetic relations other than 2D Parikh counts, stochastic observation and real-world transfer are out of scope. Postscore amendments must be labeled exploratory and must not be backdated as confirmatory. H4 success cannot imply OAIE.
