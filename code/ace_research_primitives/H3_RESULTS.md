### Results across 48 new target DFAs

Each task has an independently sampled 6-, 8-, 10-, or 12-state target DFA; 63 fully labeled train words of lengths 0–5, 192 diagnostic validation words of lengths 6–7, and 256 disjoint length-OOD holdout words of lengths 14–20. Source target transitions are not seen by the learner. Exactly one train label is inverted in the noisy arm. Both arms reuse the same 24,528 unique synthetic evaluator labels across the 48 worlds; original train-label purchases = 3,024. Target generation uses source topology and training-only class balance, not any holdout statistics.

| Metric | Clean label | One corrupted training label |
|---|---:|---:|
| Induced holdout correct / 12,288 | **9,985** (81.2581%) | **9,497** (77.2868%) |
| Optimistic per-task best of controls | 9,161 (74.5524%) | 9,185 (74.7477%) |
| Absolute gain vs control oracle | **+6.7057 pp** | **+2.5391 pp** |
| Tasks >=90% holdout | **16/48** | **11/48** |
| Proposed state-merge attempts | 2,476 | 3,946 |

Noise decreases pooled held-out accuracy by **3.9714 percentage points**. This meets the noise-degradation subthreshold of <=5pp but fails the separate requirement of >=36/48 good noisy worlds.

## Independent within-team verification

It reconstructs the 48 task generation attempts, labels and holdouts with a separate reference interpreter, replays serialized induced automata, reimplements the fixed library and both other controls, and recomputes all scores and gates. Result: **48 worlds / 24,528 labels / 1,152 (world × arm × model × split) score cells verified**. ## Interpretation and open falsifiers

H1/H2 showed that fixed classical state merging can infer reusable data-induced transition graphs on small regular languages; H3 falsifies the stronger claim that this performance reliably scales to more complex hidden state machines or occasional mislabeled observations under the same 63-label budget.

This is **not** novel invention of a general computational primitive class. The finite automaton representation and RPNI-like merge mechanism are still researcher-imposed. The 48 worlds are synthetic, selected conditionally on training statistics, and their examples are not statistically independent samples of arbitrary intelligence tasks. Separate source-state count is only a complexity proxy; many DFAs may minimize to fewer states. Controls share observation count but are not fully search-compute matched. No third-party evaluator, exact-GitHub-head CI, stochastic tasks, higher alphabet, or counter/stack representation discovery has yet passed. Uncertainty remains substantial; no OAIE claim.
