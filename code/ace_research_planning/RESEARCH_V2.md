# ACE R6 exploratory v2 — structural planning and route-independent surprises

Status: **PUBLIC SYNTHETIC / RESEARCH EXPLORATORY / NO SCIENTIFIC GO**. This is not a learned reasoning mechanism or OAIE. v1 results and its digest remain immutable and reproducible. No changes to ACE `main`, E1.4 frozen sources, or other research lanes.

## Hypothesis and preconditions

A gate-to-key dependency certificate can permit exact ordered-subgoal planning without a full search over all key masks. On tasks without certified order, full-state A* reduces action cost at the price of increased search; scarce remaining node budget should prefer feasible hierarchical paths. `forced_key_order_certificate` uses explicit visible task rules, not unseen changes or oracle answers. The strategy and threshold are researcher-authored, NOT learned from outcomes. Sufficient certificate: for k keys, boundary j is locked by key j and key j occupies column j, from a start in column zero. Thus all valid solutions must collect keys in increasing order. Segment shortest paths via A* are optimal on a static graph in this certified family; the implementation does not assert this for other graph families.

Policy `structural`: for certified tasks, plan only to the first missing required key, then goal. Otherwise, use global A* if remaining total expansion budget >= 2 × (vertices × 2^keys); use hierarchical replanning otherwise. Previously observed failed edge remains blocked in planner memory for the remainder of a rollout. Search node expansions are charged across all searches and replans, including unsuccessful searches. Switches are counted when planning modes change. This policy has no optimized hyperparameters fitted to the transfer split.

## Protocol

Reproduce from checkout:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=code python -m unittest ace_research_planning.test_planning ace_research_planning.test_study_v2 -v
PYTHONPATH=code python -m ace_research_planning.benchmark --output /tmp/r6_v1.json --limit 1800 --n 18
PYTHONPATH=code python -m ace_research_planning.study_v2 --output /tmp/r6_v2.json --n 24
```

The new separate `study_v2.py` uses seeds `dev2=2000..2023` and `transfer2=9500..9523`, not the previously evaluated v1 seeds. Each task has three conditions: unchanged/static, the original targeted surprise (chosen from the fixed planner's route), and **neutral** surprise (uniform seeded permutation of physical edges, first remaining-solvable edge, independent of any planner's route). Exact experiments run six old methods plus structural at caps 60, 120, 1800: 2 families × 24 worlds × 3 conditions × 3 caps × 7 methods = **3,024 runs**. Offline Dijkstra reference is implemented independently from the planner search function and uses physical edge weights and key-gate semantics; it is only an ex post cost lower bound. The neutral protocol does not guarantee any planner will attempt the blocked edge; report encounters separately to avoid conflating recovery with never meeting a change.

Results carry per-run seed, condition, cap, method, action cost, search expansions, heuristic calls, failure/recovery status, strategy choices, and an aggregate deterministic digest. The full per-run JSON is regenerated by the CLI; committed `results_v2_summary.json` contains all grouped statistics and the full-run SHA256 receipt. v1 digest is unchanged: `d13e42e05f2385075218d9dab9f659c3425898381fd61cb93bd1143f96cc909f`.

## Results (full-run canonical digest `6ae6b52754bd844a4e01295ca3fa02ff008e3edd196f11867535b874ec750873`)

Transfer2 / neutral edge, 24 worlds, cap 1800:

| Method | Success | Encounters | Recovery among encounters | Mean expansions | Cost / hindsight offline optimum (successful only) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hierarchical replan | 24 | 6 | 6 | 54.33 | 1.0567 |
| Adaptive hierarchy→A* | 24 | 6 | 6 | 69.25 | 1.0567 |
| Structural | 24 | 7 | 7 | 159.92 | 1.0125 |
| Full A* | 24 | 7 | 7 | 178.54 | 1.0125 |

Transfer2 / targeted edge, 24 worlds, cap 1800:

| Method | Success | Mean expansions | Cost / lower bound |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hierarchical replan | 24 | 61.88 | 1.0485 |
| Adaptive hierarchy→A* | 24 | 164.08 | 1.0077 |
| Structural | 24 | 261.21 | 1.0077 |
| Full A* | 24 | 312.00 | 1.0077 |

Under cap 60 on transfer2/neutral: hierarchical replan **17/24** successes, structural **16/24**, full A* **5/24**. On transfer2/targeted: hierarchical **11/24**, structural **12/24**, old adaptive **5/24**. Structural improves average path cost in high-cap transfer tasks but is **not Pareto dominant** and can lose successes under tight caps. Changes to the hidden edge do not affect original search information until an attempted action fails. In the neutral condition only 6–7/24 planners actually encounter the edge; 24/24 completion alone is not proof of recovery.

## Interpretation and falsification

* Replanning and persistent blocked-edge memory are sufficient for recovery in this controlled single-change environment. They are not evidence of self-discovered strategy.
* The certificate itself is an explicit hand-authored structural proof template. It fails closed outside ordered gate-key systems and cannot extrapolate to general causal planning.
* Higher path optimality can require substantially higher search work. Nodes and heuristic calls are counted, but these do not measure equal wall time or equal computation: MST heuristics are more expensive than simple greedy.
* Ex post oracle routes presume foreknowledge of closures and are **not** feasible policy oracles. The cost ratio is diagnostic only.
* Both split families and seeded surprise sampler are public and authored by the experimenter; `transfer2` is **not** an independently blinded holdout. Because the study was designed after inspecting v1 OOD, this is exploratory replication, not confirmatory generalization.
* Small instance scale, single undirected static closure, known deterministic key mechanics, perfect memory, no task transfer learning and no external world validation. A mixed-mechanism pre-registered blind study, budget-in-FLOPs benchmarking and independent review must precede scientific acceptance.

**Release decision: HOLD / draft PR**. No evidence supports claiming autonomous intelligence evolution, learned reasoning, broad compositional generalization or reliable advantage over a compute-matched oracle/planner.
